Philosophy

A note from the maker.

Most apps today feel like they want something from you.

  • Your attention.
  • Your data.
  • Your time.

Lychee is inspired by how software used to feel, before every product was designed to extract more from you.

You downloaded something useful. It worked. It stayed yours.

That is the promise behind Lychee: a beautiful notes app with no tracking, no hidden agenda, and no reason to take more than it needs.

Just a simple place to write, made to stay free and yours.

Let's love Lychee.

Local-first

Your notes,
on your machine.

SQLite. Your notes live in a plain SQLite file on your machine — an open format, easy to back up or migrate.

Private. No telemetry, no analytics, no hidden data collection.

Offline. Works without a network. There's no online mode to fall back from.
