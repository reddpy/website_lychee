Philosophy

Built to be used every day,
for a long time.

Lychee is the notes app we wanted to use ourselves. It opens instantly, gets out of the way, and treats your writing like yours.

Every block is a place to write. Every shortcut is bound. Every note is yours to organize the way you think — five levels of nesting, tabs for side-by-side, a single emoji for an icon if you want one.

Your notes live on your computer as one SQLite file. You can read it, back it up, sync it however you like, or never sync it at all. No server. No cloud tier. Yours, the way notes should be.

Local-first

Your notes,
on your machine.

Lychee is local-first. In practice, that means three things.

One. Your notes are a file on your computer. SQLite, an open format, no proprietary container. If Lychee disappears tomorrow, your writing doesn't.

Two. Nothing leaves your machine. No telemetry. No analytics. No background sync to a server we operate.

Three. The app works offline because it only ever works offline. There's no online mode to fall back from.

It's the only honest way to make a tool you'd trust with a decade of writing.
