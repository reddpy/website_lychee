Already keeping notes somewhere else?
Here's the honest case for making the switch.

  • Coming from Notion?

    Trade the cloud, the login, and the lag for one quiet file that lives on your machine. It opens the instant you do, online or not.

  • Coming from Obsidian?

    The same local-first soul and Markdown export, in a calm app that's lovely out of the box. No plugin assembly required.

  • Coming from Apple Notes?

    Everything you like about keeping it simple. Now on Mac, Windows, and Linux, fully open source, with no account to sign into.
