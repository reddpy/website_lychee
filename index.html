Creator's Note

Apps today feel like they want something from you.

  • Your attention.
  • Your data.
  • Your time.
🌸

Lychee is inspired by how software used to feel — useful, quiet, and yours.

  • No tracking.
  • No hidden agenda.
  • No reason to take more than it needs.

Just a beautiful place to write, made to stay free and yours.

Let's Love Lychee

Local-first

Your notes,
your machine.

  1. 01

    Easy to keep. Your notes are saved on your computer in a simple, portable file.

  2. 02

    Private by default. No tracking, no analytics, no hidden data collection.

  3. 03

    Works offline. Lychee keeps working without the internet, because your notes should not depend on a server.
